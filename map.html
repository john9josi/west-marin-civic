Where West Marin Civic looks

Road closures and power outages use one rectangle. Active fires use a larger rectangle. Weather alerts use the whole National Weather Service zone for the coastal North Bay. Point Reyes, Inverness, and Inverness Park sit inside all three. The coordinates for each filter are in the FAQ.

That rectangle runs from south of Stinson Beach to just past Valley Ford, and from offshore of the Point Reyes headlands to east of Nicasio. It is about 34 miles north to south and 27 miles east to west. A 511 event inside it is shown only when the road name is one of: Hwy 1 North, Hwy 1 South, Sir Francis Drake Blvd, Bear Valley Rd, Point Reyes-Petaluma Rd, Platform Bridge Rd, Nicasio Valley Rd, Marshall-Petaluma Rd, Tomales-Petaluma Rd, Fallon-Two Rock Rd, or Valley Ford Rd. PG&E outages use the same rectangle and do not need a road name.

Weather uses NWS zone CAZ505, Coastal North Bay Including Point Reyes National Seashore. The numbers above are how far that polygon reaches. They are not a box the app clips to. An alert anywhere in the zone changes the status, including the Sonoma coast north of Valley Ford and a separate piece of the zone around the Marin Headlands. Nicasio, Novato, Fairfax, and Petaluma are outside CAZ505 even though they fall inside the road rectangle.

Where does Highway 1 switch from North to South?

Hwy 1 North

A Highway 1 event at 38.055°N, or any latitude north of it, is Hwy 1 North. South of that line is Hwy 1 South. Olema, at 38.042°N, is on the south side. The line is only used after the event has already passed the road rectangle.

Road and power rectangle: WM_BBOX in src/lib.js. Fire rectangle: the WFIGS envelope in index.html and worker.js. Weather zone: api.weather.gov zone CAZ505. Place dots are OpenStreetMap localities, except Inverness, which is the post office on Sir Francis Drake Blvd.
